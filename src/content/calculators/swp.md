### What is it?
The mirror image of SIP. You invest a corpus and withdraw a fixed amount every month, while the remaining balance continues earning returns. Used primarily for retirement income — your money keeps working while you spend from it.

### The Formula
Month-by-month depletion: Balance(n) = Balance(n-1) × (1 + monthly return) – monthly withdrawal.

### Why it Matters
A ₹1 crore corpus earning 8% while you withdraw ₹50,000/month lasts over 30 years. The same corpus withdrawing ₹1 lakh/month lasts less than 12. The rate of withdrawal relative to returns is everything.

### How to Use it
Enter your total corpus, monthly withdrawal amount, and expected return. The chart shows how your balance depletes over time — and when it runs out if withdrawals exceed sustainable levels.

### Assumptions
Returns are constant — actual returns vary. Sequence-of-returns risk (bad early years) can significantly shorten corpus life. Illustrative only.
