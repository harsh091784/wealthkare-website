### What is it?
India Post offers a family of government-backed small savings schemes — NSC, KVP, Monthly Income Scheme, and Time Deposits — each designed for different needs: tax-saving growth, simple doubling, monthly income, or fixed-term deposits, all with sovereign guarantee.

### The Formula
Varies by scheme — NSC and Time Deposits compound (annually or quarterly), KVP simply doubles your money over a fixed period, and MIS pays a fixed monthly income on your principal.

### Why it Matters
These schemes offer bank-independent, government-guaranteed options accessible through any post office — useful for conservative investors, especially in smaller towns where the post office network is often more accessible than private banks.

### How to Use it
Select the scheme, enter your investment amount, and see the projected outcome.

### Note
All interest rates on post office schemes are set by the Government of India and revised quarterly. Interest is taxable as per your slab, except where specified (NSC has partial 80C benefit). Illustrative only.
