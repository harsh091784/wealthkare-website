### What is it?
The National Pension System is a government-backed retirement savings scheme open to all Indian citizens between 18 and 70 years of age. You contribute monthly during your working years, the corpus grows in market-linked funds, and at retirement you receive a lump sum plus a monthly pension from the annuity portion.

### The Formula
FV of monthly contributions to age 60 using standard SIP compounding. At maturity: 40% of corpus is mandatorily used to purchase an annuity (monthly pension); 60% is available as a tax-free lump sum. Monthly pension = Annuity corpus × annuity rate ÷ 12.

### Why it Matters
NPS offers one of the best tax-saving combinations available — deductions under 80C (up to ₹1.5L), an additional ₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B), and a tax-free 60% lump sum at maturity. For salaried individuals, it is one of the most tax-efficient retirement tools available.

### How to Use it
Enter your current age, monthly NPS contribution, expected fund return during accumulation, and the annuity rate at maturity. The calculator shows your corpus at 60, the lump sum you can withdraw, and your estimated monthly pension.

### Assumptions
Returns during accumulation and annuity rates are assumed constant. Actual NPS fund returns vary by fund manager and asset class chosen. Annuity rates at maturity depend on the annuity provider selected. Illustrative only.
