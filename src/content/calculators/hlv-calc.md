### What is it?
Human Life Value is the economic worth of a person's future earning potential — the amount of life insurance needed to replace the income they would have provided to their family had they lived to retirement.

### The Formula
The present value of all future income (growing annually) that the person would have earned until retirement, discounted back to today's value.

### Why it Matters
Most people are underinsured because they pick a round number ("1 crore sounds like a lot") rather than calculating what their family would actually need to replace their income for 20+ years. HLV gives a defensible, income-based number.

### How to Use it
Enter your current income, years remaining to retirement, expected income growth, and any existing cover. The Cover Gap shows exactly how much additional term insurance you need.

### Assumptions
Assumes income would have grown steadily; does not account for one-time future expenses (children's education, marriage) which should be added separately. Illustrative only.
