### What is it?
A balance transfer moves your existing home loan to a new lender offering a lower interest rate, potentially saving significant interest over the remaining tenure — a decision worth evaluating whenever rate gaps widen.

### The Formula
EMI and total interest are calculated at both the current and the new rate for the remaining tenure; the processing fee for the new loan is subtracted to determine the true net saving.

### Why it Matters
A 1% rate reduction on a large, long-tenure loan can save lakhs — but the decision must account for processing fees, and other switching costs. This calculator shows exactly when the switch breaks even and starts saving you money.

### How to Use it
Enter your current loan details, the new rate on offer, and the processing fee for the transfer. The break-even period tells you how quickly the switch pays for itself.

### Assumptions
Does not include other charges like legal/technical fees some lenders levy. Compare full cost structures, not just headline rates, before switching. Illustrative only.
