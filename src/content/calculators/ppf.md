### What is it?
The Public Provident Fund is a government-backed, long-term savings scheme with a 15-year lock-in, extendable in 5-year blocks. It offers a sovereign guarantee on both principal and interest, complete tax exemption (EEE status — exempt on investment, exempt on interest, exempt on maturity), and one of the most reliable risk-free returns available in India.

### The Formula
PPF compounds annually. Interest is calculated on the minimum balance between the 5th and last day of each month — so deposits made before the 5th earn interest for that month. FV = sum of each annual contribution compounded at the PPF rate for the remaining years.

### Why it Matters
PPF is one of the very few instruments in India with full EEE tax status. The interest is completely tax-free, the maturity amount is tax-free, and contributions are eligible for 80C deduction. For conservative investors, it is the bedrock of tax-efficient savings.

### How to Use it
Enter your annual contribution (maximum ₹1.5 lakhs per year), the duration (15 years minimum, extendable), and the current PPF interest rate. The calculator shows your maturity value, total invested, and total interest earned.

### Note
The PPF interest rate is set by the Government of India and revised quarterly. The current rate is shown as default — update it if the rate changes. Illustrative only.
