### What is it?
Income protection cover — often through disability insurance or an income replacement rider — ensures your family's monthly expenses are met if you're unable to earn due to illness, accident, or disability, distinct from life insurance which only pays on death.

### The Formula
Multiply your monthly expenses by the number of months of protection desired, then subtract any existing savings that could bridge the gap.

### Why it Matters
Life insurance protects against death, but the more common risk — a serious illness or disability that stops income without ending life — is often completely uninsured. Income protection fills this gap, which term insurance does not cover.

### How to Use it
Enter your monthly expenses, desired protection period, and existing emergency savings. The result shows the additional cover needed.

### Assumptions
Product availability and terms for disability/income protection riders vary by insurer. This is a planning estimate, not a policy quote. Illustrative only.
