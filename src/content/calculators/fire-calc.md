### What is it?
FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) is a framework for calculating the exact corpus needed to live off your investments indefinitely — freeing you from dependence on a salary, whether or not you choose to stop working.

### The Formula
Your FIRE Number is your annual expenses divided by a safe withdrawal rate (typically 3–4%) — the inverse of the classic "4% rule." This is the corpus size that can theoretically sustain your expenses forever through returns alone.

### Why it Matters
FIRE reframes retirement planning around a specific number rather than a specific age. Someone spending ₹6 lakhs annually needs roughly ₹1.5–2 crores (at a 3.5–4% withdrawal rate) to become financially independent — a concrete target that turns "someday" into a plan with a monthly number attached.

### How to Use it
Enter your current annual expenses, age, target FIRE age, and existing savings. The calculator shows your FIRE Number and the monthly SIP required to reach it on schedule.

### Assumptions
The safe withdrawal rate assumes a diversified portfolio and market conditions similar to historical averages — it is not guaranteed. Early retirees face a longer withdrawal horizon, which increases sequence-of-returns risk. Illustrative only.
