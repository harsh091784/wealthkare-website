### What is it?
A Unit Linked Insurance Plan combines life insurance and market-linked investment in one product. This calculator compares your likely ULIP outcome against the alternative of buying a separate term plan and investing the remaining premium directly in mutual funds.

### The Formula
The ULIP fund value compounds at the expected return, reduced annually by mortality and administrative charges. The BTI alternative assumes the same total premium, minus an estimated low-cost term insurance premium, invested in a mutual fund without ULIP-style charges.

### Why it Matters
ULIPs bundle insurance and investment, but usually at a higher combined cost than buying each separately. Separating the two — a pure term plan for protection, and a mutual fund for growth — very often produces a larger final corpus for the same monthly outlay.

### How to Use it
Enter your ULIP premium, term, expected return, and charge structure. Compare the two final values side by side before deciding.

### Note
Actual ULIP charges vary significantly by insurer and product — check your specific policy's charge structure. This comparison is illustrative, not a recommendation against any specific product. Illustrative only.
