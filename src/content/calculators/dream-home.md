### What is it?
A calculator that plans your home purchase — projecting what the property will cost when you're ready to buy, the down payment you'll need, and the monthly investment to build it.

### The Formula
Property prices are inflated at the appreciation rate to your target year; the down payment portion is then converted into a required monthly SIP.

### Why it Matters
The home you can see today will cost more when you're ready. A ₹50 lakh property appreciating at 6% costs ₹67 lakhs in 5 years — meaning your ₹10 lakh down payment target is actually ₹13.4 lakhs. Planning for the future price, not today's, is the difference between buying on schedule and postponing for years.

### How to Use it
Enter the current price of the kind of property you want, your target timeline, and expected returns on your savings. The calculator gives you a concrete monthly number.

### Assumptions
Property appreciation varies significantly by city and locality. Loan eligibility for the remaining amount is calculated separately — see the Loan Eligibility calculator.
