### What is it?
A calculator that classifies your investment gain as Long-Term Capital Gain (LTCG) or Short-Term Capital Gain (STCG) based on your holding period, then computes the tax liability using the correct rate for each asset type under current Indian tax law.

### The Formula
Holding period = Sale date – Purchase date. Classification and tax rates post-Budget 2024: Equity MF and Stocks — STCG (held under 1 year): 20%; LTCG (held 1 year or more, gains above ₹1.25 lakhs): 12.5% without indexation. Debt MF — taxed as per income slab regardless of holding period (post-April 2023 amendment). Property and Gold — LTCG (held 2 years or more): 12.5% without indexation (post-July 2024 Budget).

### Why it Matters
Selling at the wrong time can trigger significantly higher tax. An equity investment held for 364 days versus 366 days can double your tax rate. This calculator helps you plan the timing of your redemptions to minimise tax impact.

### How to Use it
Select the asset type, enter purchase price, sale price, purchase date, and sale date. The calculator auto-classifies the gain and applies the correct current tax rate.

### Note
Tax laws changed significantly in Budget 2024. Rates shown reflect post-Budget 2024 rules. Tax laws are subject to further change — consult a tax advisor for your specific situation. Illustrative only.
