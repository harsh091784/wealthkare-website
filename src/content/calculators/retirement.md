### What is it?
The most important financial calculation most people never do. This calculator answers two questions: how much will you need at retirement, and how much must you save each month to get there?

### The Formula
Step 1 — inflate your current monthly expenses to their value at retirement: Expenses at retirement = Current expenses × (1 + inflation)^years to retire. Step 2 — calculate the corpus needed to sustain those expenses through your retirement years using an annuity formula. Step 3 — back-calculate the monthly SIP needed today to build that corpus.

### Why it Matters
Inflation is the silent threat to retirement. ₹75,000/month today becomes ₹2.4 lakhs/month in 25 years at 6% inflation. The corpus needed to fund that lifestyle for 25 years of retirement runs into crores — and the earlier you start building it, the less you need to contribute each month.

### How to Use it
Enter your current age, target retirement age, current monthly expenses, expected inflation, post-retirement return, and how many years you expect to spend in retirement. The calculator shows your required retirement corpus and your required monthly SIP to reach it.

### Assumptions
Inflation and returns are assumed constant. Actual outcomes will vary. This is a planning estimate — revisit it annually and after any major life change. Illustrative only; not a guarantee.
