### What is it?
A calculator showing how much interest you save and how much sooner you become debt-free by making a prepayment on an existing loan — whether a one-time lump sum or extra monthly payments.

### The Formula
The prepayment is applied to the outstanding principal, and the amortization schedule is recalculated — either shortening the tenure (same EMI) or lowering the EMI (same tenure).

### Why it Matters
Because home loan interest is front-loaded, prepayments made early in the loan save disproportionately more interest than the same prepayment made later. A ₹5 lakh prepayment in year 2 of a 20-year loan can save lakhs in interest and cut years off the tenure.

### How to Use it
Enter your outstanding loan details and the prepayment you're considering. Choose whether to reduce your tenure or your EMI, and see the interest saved either way.

### Assumptions
Most Indian banks do not charge prepayment penalties on floating-rate home loans (per RBI guidelines). Check your specific loan agreement for applicable charges. Illustrative only.
