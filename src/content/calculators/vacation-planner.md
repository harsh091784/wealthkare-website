### What is it?
A calculator for planning that dream vacation — international trip, family holiday, or milestone celebration — funded through disciplined saving rather than credit cards or loans.

### The Formula
Today's trip cost inflated to your travel year, converted into a required monthly SIP.

### Why it Matters
Travel funded by credit card EMIs costs 30–40% more after interest. The same trip, planned 2–3 years ahead through a simple SIP, costs you exactly what it should — and you return home without debt waiting.

### How to Use it
Estimate what your trip costs today, set your travel year, and start the monthly SIP. For horizons under 3 years, prefer debt or liquid funds over equity.

### Assumptions
Travel inflation (flights, hotels) often runs above CPI. Short-horizon investments should prioritize capital safety over returns. Illustrative only.
