### What is it?
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a government savings scheme exclusively for the girl child, offering one of the highest interest rates among small savings schemes along with complete tax exemption — a rare EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) status.

### The Formula
Contributions are made annually for 15 years from account opening. The account continues to earn interest (with no further deposits required) until the girl reaches 21, or until she marries after 18, whichever is earlier.

### Why it Matters
SSY combines the highest small-savings rate available in India with full tax exemption on investment, interest, and maturity — making it one of the most efficient ways to build a dedicated corpus for a daughter's education or marriage.

### How to Use it
Enter your planned annual contribution, your daughter's current age, and the current SSY rate. The calculator projects the maturity value when she turns 21.

### Note
The account can only be opened for a girl child before she turns 10, by a parent or legal guardian. Maximum two accounts per family (three in case of twins). Interest rate is revised quarterly by the Government of India. Illustrative only.
