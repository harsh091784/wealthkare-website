### What is it?
The purest demonstration of compounding — how a single sum of money grows over time purely through reinvested returns, with no additional contributions.

### The Formula
Future Value = Principal × (1 + rate/compounding frequency)^(frequency × years).

### Why it Matters
Compounding is often called the eighth wonder of the world for a reason — the difference between 10 and 30 years of growth at the same rate isn't triple, it's often 10-15 times larger, because growth compounds on growth. Seeing this curve is the best argument for starting early.

### How to Use it
Enter an amount, a return rate, and a duration. Watch how dramatically the curve steepens in the later years — that's compounding accelerating.

### Assumptions
Assumes no withdrawals and a constant return rate, which real investments rarely provide exactly — but the underlying principle holds over long periods. Illustrative only.
