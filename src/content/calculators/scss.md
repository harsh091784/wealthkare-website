### What is it?
The Senior Citizen Savings Scheme is a government-backed savings scheme exclusively for individuals aged 60 and above (55+ for retired defence personnel), offering one of the highest guaranteed interest rates among fixed-income instruments in India, with quarterly interest payouts.

### The Formula
Simple interest, paid out quarterly: Principal × Rate ÷ 4 every quarter — not compounded, since interest is paid out rather than reinvested.

### Why it Matters
For retirees seeking a predictable income stream with sovereign backing, SCSS offers rates typically higher than bank FDs, along with the safety of a government scheme. The maximum investment limit is ₹30 lakhs per individual.

### How to Use it
Enter your investment amount (up to ₹30 lakhs) and the current SCSS rate. The calculator shows your guaranteed quarterly income.

### Note
SCSS interest rate is set by the Government of India and revised quarterly. Interest is fully taxable as per your income slab. Premature withdrawal is permitted with a penalty. Illustrative only.
