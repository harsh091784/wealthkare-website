### What is it?
Personal loans are unsecured loans for any purpose — medical expenses, weddings, travel, debt consolidation — carrying no collateral requirement but significantly higher interest rates than secured loans.

### The Formula
Standard EMI calculation, but at unsecured-loan rates which run considerably higher than home or car loans due to the absence of collateral.

### Why it Matters
At 14-18% interest, a personal loan is one of the costliest ways to borrow. This calculator makes the true cost visible before committing — often revealing that drawing from savings or a lower-cost alternative (like a loan against securities) is far cheaper.

### How to Use it
Enter the amount, rate, and tenure. If the total interest shown feels high, consider whether a secured borrowing option might serve the same need at a lower cost.

### Assumptions
Rates vary significantly by lender and credit profile. Processing fees (often 1-3%) not included. Illustrative only.
