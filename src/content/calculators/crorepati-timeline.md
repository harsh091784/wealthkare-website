### What is it?
The Crorepati Timeline calculator answers a simple but powerful question: given your monthly SIP and expected return, when exactly will you reach ₹1 crore? Instead of asking how much you'll accumulate in a fixed period, it solves for the time it takes to hit your target.

### The Formula
It solves for the number of months (n) in the SIP future value equation, given a target of ₹1 crore: n = log(1 + (Target × r) / P) / log(1 + r), where Target is ₹1 crore, r is your monthly rate of return, and P is your monthly SIP.

### Why it Matters
₹1 crore can feel like an abstract, distant goal. Seeing it as a concrete date changes everything. A ₹10,000 monthly SIP at 12% reaches ₹1 crore in roughly 20 years — but ₹20,000 a month cuts that to about 15 years, and ₹30,000 gets you there in around 12. Watching the timeline shrink as you increase your contribution is one of the most motivating things in personal finance.

### How to Use it
Enter your monthly SIP amount and your expected annual return. The calculator shows the exact year and month you'll cross ₹1 crore. Adjust the SIP up or down to see how much sooner — or later — you reach the milestone.

### Assumptions
Returns are assumed constant, though real markets fluctuate. The timeline is a projection, not a promise — but the underlying principle, that higher contributions and earlier starts dramatically shorten the journey, holds regardless of the exact rate. Illustrative only. Not a guarantee of returns.
