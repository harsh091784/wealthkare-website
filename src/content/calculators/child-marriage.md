### What is it?
A goal calculator that estimates the future cost of your child's marriage and the monthly investment needed to fund it without borrowing or breaking other investments.

### The Formula
Inflate today's estimated cost to the marriage year, then back-calculate the monthly SIP needed to accumulate that amount.

### Why it Matters
Marriage costs in India routinely run into tens of lakhs, and they arrive on a fixed timeline you cannot postpone. Funding it through a planned SIP over 15–20 years costs a fraction per month of what a last-minute loan would.

### How to Use it
Enter your child's age, expected marriage age, what a wedding of your standard costs today, and expected returns. Start the SIP; review every few years as costs and plans evolve.

### Assumptions
7% inflation default for wedding costs. Returns assumed constant. Illustrative only.
