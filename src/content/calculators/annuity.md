### What is it?
An annuity converts a lumpsum into a guaranteed regular income stream — you hand over a corpus once, and receive fixed payments for a defined period or for life, depending on the annuity type chosen.

### The Formula
Payout = Corpus × Annuity Rate, divided across your chosen frequency (monthly, quarterly, or annual).

### Why it Matters
Annuities are the primary way NPS and pension corpuses convert into retirement income. Understanding the payout you'll actually receive — often lower than expected once you see it in monthly terms — is essential before committing a large corpus to an annuity provider.

### How to Use it
Enter your corpus, the annuity rate quoted by the provider, and your preferred payout frequency. Compare the monthly payout against your expected retirement expenses.

### Assumptions
Annuity rates vary by provider and product type (immediate vs deferred, with or without return of purchase price). Rates shown are illustrative. Always compare multiple annuity providers before committing.
