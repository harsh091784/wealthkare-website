### What is it?
A calculator showing your monthly repayment for a car loan, along with the total interest cost over the loan's life.

### The Formula
EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n – 1] (monthly rate, monthly installments).

### Why it Matters
Car loans carry higher interest rates than home loans and depreciate against a depreciating asset — the combination makes total cost awareness critical. A ₹5 lakh loan at 9% over 5 years costs over ₹1.2 lakhs in interest alone.

### How to Use it
Enter the loan amount, rate, and tenure. Compare the total interest cost against a larger down payment to see the savings.

### Assumptions
EMI on reducing balance basis. Processing fees, insurance bundling, and prepayment charges not included. Illustrative only.
