### What is it?
A side-by-side comparison of your tax liability under India's Old Tax Regime (with deductions) and the New Tax Regime (lower slabs, fewer deductions) for the current financial year. Helps you decide which regime saves you more tax.

### The Formula
Calculates taxable income under each regime by applying the respective deductions (80C, 80D, HRA, NPS, standard deduction), then applies the correct tax slab rates for FY2025-26 to each, adds a 4% health and education cess, and computes the final tax liability.

### Why it Matters
The right regime choice depends on your income level and how many deductions you claim. Investors who max out 80C, 80D, and NPS typically save more under the Old Regime. Those with fewer deductions often pay less under the New Regime. The difference can run into tens of thousands of rupees annually.

### How to Use it
Enter your annual income, standard deduction, 80C investments, 80D health insurance premium, HRA exemption, NPS 80CCD(1B) contribution, and other deductions. The calculator shows your tax under both regimes side by side and highlights which one saves you more.

### Assumptions
Based on FY2025-26 tax slabs and rates. Surcharge applicable for incomes above ₹50 lakhs is included. Tax laws are subject to change — consult a qualified tax expert for your specific situation. Illustrative only.
