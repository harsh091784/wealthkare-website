### What is it?
A fast, simplified version of the full tax regime comparison — for when you just want a quick directional answer without entering every deduction.

### The Formula
Same underlying FY2025-26 slab calculation as the full Income Tax calculator, but using only income and 80C as inputs, assuming no other deductions.

### Why it Matters
Not everyone wants to fill in six fields for a quick sense-check. This gives an instant directional answer, with a clear path to the detailed calculator for anyone who wants precision.

### How to Use it
Enter your income, 80C investment amount, and employment type. For a complete picture including HRA, health insurance, and NPS, use the full calculator linked below.

### Assumptions
Based on FY2025-26 tax slabs. This simplified version assumes no deductions beyond 80C and standard deduction — the tax slabs are subject to official revision. Illustrative only.
