### What is it?
A wealth-target calculator that shows how long it will take to reach ₹1 crore given your monthly SIP and expected returns — or how much you need to invest monthly to become a crorepati by a target date.

### The Formula
Solves for n (time) in the SIP formula given a target FV of ₹1 crore: n = log(1 + FV×r/P) / log(1+r). Where FV = ₹1 crore, r = monthly rate, P = monthly SIP.

### Why it Matters
₹1 crore sounds distant. But ₹10,000/month at 12% gets you there in about 20 years. ₹20,000/month gets you there in 15. Seeing the timeline makes the goal concrete and motivating.

### How to Use it
Enter your monthly SIP and expected return. The result shows when you'll reach ₹1 crore — the exact year and month. Adjust the SIP to see how starting earlier or investing more changes the timeline.

### Assumptions
Returns assumed constant. Actual results will vary with market conditions. Illustrative only.
