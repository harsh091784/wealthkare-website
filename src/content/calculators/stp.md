### What is it?
A Systematic Transfer Plan moves a fixed amount every month from one mutual fund to another — typically from a debt fund to an equity fund. You park a lumpsum in a low-risk fund and transfer gradually into equity, getting rupee-cost averaging on the way in.

### The Formula
Each month: source balance grows at the source fund rate, then the transfer amount moves out. The target fund receives the transfer and compounds at the target rate.

### Why it Matters
STP solves the biggest dilemma of lumpsum investing — deploying a large amount into equity at the wrong time. Instead of investing ₹10 lakhs into equity in one day, an STP spreads it over 12–24 months, reducing timing risk.

### How to Use it
Enter your lumpsum corpus, monthly transfer amount, both funds' expected returns, and the duration. The chart shows your money migrating from safety to growth.

### Assumptions
Returns assumed constant for both funds. Exit loads and taxation on transfers (each transfer is a redemption) are not included — consult a tax advisor. Illustrative only.
