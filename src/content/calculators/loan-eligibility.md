### What is it?
Before applying for a loan, banks assess how much you can eligibly borrow based on your income and existing obligations. This calculator estimates that number using standard banking norms.

### The Formula
Banks typically cap total EMI obligations at 40-50% of net monthly income. This calculator subtracts your existing EMIs from that cap, then reverse-calculates the loan amount that fits the remaining eligible EMI at your chosen rate and tenure.

### Why it Matters
Knowing your eligibility before house-hunting or car-shopping prevents wasted time pursuing properties or vehicles beyond your approved range — and helps you understand how existing debt is limiting new borrowing capacity.

### How to Use it
Enter your monthly income, existing EMIs, loan type, and expected rate/tenure. The result is an estimate of your maximum loan eligibility.

### Assumptions
Actual eligibility varies by lender, credit score, employment type, and other factors. This is an estimate using standard industry norms, not a loan approval. Illustrative only.
