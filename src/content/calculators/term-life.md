### What is it?
A calculator estimating how much term life insurance cover you need — combining income replacement, outstanding debts, and future family obligations into a single practical number.

### The Formula
Multiply your annual income by the years your family would need replacement income, add any outstanding loans (so they don't inherit your debt), add a buffer per dependent for future education or marriage costs, then subtract existing cover.

### Why it Matters
Term insurance is the most cost-effective way to protect your family's financial future, but most Indians are dramatically underinsured — the average cover is a fraction of what's actually needed. This calculator gives you a number grounded in your actual obligations, not a guess.

### How to Use it
Enter your income, debts, dependents, and existing cover. The result is your recommended additional term cover.

### Assumptions
This is a planning estimate — insurers assess actual eligibility based on income, age, and health. Term insurance should be reviewed every 3-5 years or after major life events. Illustrative only.
