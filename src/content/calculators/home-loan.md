### What is it?
A Home Loan EMI calculator shows you your monthly repayment amount, the total interest you will pay over the loan's life, and a year-by-year breakdown of how much of each payment goes toward principal versus interest.

### The Formula
EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n – 1], where P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12), n = total number of monthly installments.

### Why it Matters
A ₹50 lakh home loan at 8.5% for 20 years results in a total repayment of over ₹1.05 crores — more than double the loan. The amortization table shows you exactly how the interest burden is front-loaded: in the early years, most of your EMI goes toward interest, not principal. Understanding this helps you decide when and how to make prepayments.

### How to Use it
Enter your loan amount, interest rate, and tenure. The calculator shows your monthly EMI, total interest payable, and total amount payable. The amortization table breaks this down year by year.

### Assumptions
EMI is calculated on a reducing balance basis, which is the standard for home loans in India. Interest rate is assumed constant — floating rate loans will vary. Prepayment charges and processing fees are not included. Illustrative only.
