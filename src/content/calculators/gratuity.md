### What is it?
Gratuity is a lump-sum benefit paid by an employer to an employee who has completed 5 or more years of continuous service, as a token of appreciation for long-term service, under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.

### The Formula
(15 days' salary for every completed year of service) — calculated as 15 × Last Drawn Basic+DA × Years of Service, divided by 26 (the standard number of working days in a month).

### Why it Matters
Gratuity is often an overlooked component of retirement or exit planning. For long-tenured employees, it can represent a meaningful lump sum — and knowing the expected amount helps in overall financial planning around a job transition or retirement.

### How to Use it
Enter your last drawn basic + DA and total years of service. The calculator shows your entitled gratuity amount.

### Note
Gratuity is tax-exempt up to ₹20 lakhs for employees covered under the Payment of Gratuity Act. Minimum eligibility is 5 years of continuous service (with some exceptions for death or disability). Illustrative only.
