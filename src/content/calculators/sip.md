### What is it?
A Systematic Investment Plan lets you invest a fixed amount in a mutual fund every month — automatically, without timing the market. You build wealth steadily through rupee-cost averaging: when markets are low, your money buys more units; when markets are high, it buys fewer. Over time, compounding does the heavy lifting.

### The Formula
FV = P × [((1+r)^n – 1) / r] × (1+r), where P = monthly SIP amount, r = monthly rate of return (annual rate ÷ 12), n = number of months.

### Why it Matters
Consistency beats timing. A ₹10,000/month SIP at 12% for 20 years grows to over ₹99 lakhs — on a total investment of just ₹24 lakhs. The rest is compounding.

### How to Use it
Enter your monthly amount, how long you plan to invest, and your expected annual return. The result shows your projected corpus, how much you invested, and how much the market added.

### Assumptions
Returns are assumed constant — actual mutual fund returns vary with market conditions. This is illustrative only; not a guarantee of future returns.
