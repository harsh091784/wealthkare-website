### What is it?
A short-horizon goal calculator for planning a vehicle purchase — what the car will cost when you buy, and the monthly savings to fund your down payment.

### The Formula
Car price inflated at ~4% annually to your purchase year; down payment converted to required monthly SIP at your expected return.

### Why it Matters
Cars are depreciating assets — the less you borrow, the less you lose. A larger planned down payment reduces your EMI burden and total interest dramatically. Saving ₹15,000 monthly for 3 years toward a down payment beats stretching a 7-year loan.

### How to Use it
Enter the current price of your target car, your timeline, and down payment percentage. Given the short horizon, consider debt or hybrid funds rather than pure equity.

### Assumptions
Shorter horizons warrant conservative return assumptions — the default 10% reflects a balanced allocation. Illustrative only.
