### What is it?
NPS Tier 2 is a voluntary savings account linked to your NPS Tier 1 account, offering the same low-cost, professionally managed fund options — but with no lock-in and no mandatory annuity requirement. You can withdraw anytime.

### The Formula
Standard compounding SIP or lumpsum growth in your chosen asset allocation (equity, corporate bonds, government securities).

### Why it Matters
Tier 2 offers NPS's low fund management costs and professional management without Tier 1's restrictions — a flexible option for investors who want NPS-style investing without locking money until retirement.

### How to Use it
Enter your monthly contribution, duration, and expected return based on your chosen asset mix.

### Assumptions
Tier 2 has no tax benefit under 80C (unlike Tier 1, except for government employees under a specific provision). Returns depend entirely on the asset allocation chosen. Illustrative only.
