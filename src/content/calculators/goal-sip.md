### What is it?
The reverse of the SIP calculator. Instead of asking "what will I accumulate?", you ask "how much must I invest monthly to reach my target?" Enter the goal, the timeline, and the expected return — the calculator works backward to give you the required monthly SIP.

### The Formula
PMT = FV × r / [((1+r)^n – 1) × (1+r)], where FV = target amount, r = monthly rate, n = months.

### Why it Matters
Every financial goal has a price in monthly discipline. Knowing exactly what that number is — for your child's education, your retirement, your home down payment — turns a vague goal into an actionable commitment.

### How to Use it
Enter your target amount, the number of years you have, and your expected return. The result is the minimum you need to invest every month starting today.

### Assumptions
Assumes a consistent monthly SIP with constant returns. Starting early dramatically reduces the required monthly amount. Illustrative only.
