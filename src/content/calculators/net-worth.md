### What is it?
A calculator that gives you a complete snapshot of your financial position — everything you own minus everything you owe — at a single point in time.

### The Formula
Add up all assets (cash, investments, property, gold, retirement funds), add up all liabilities (loans, credit card debt), and subtract.

### Why it Matters
Net worth is the single most honest measure of financial progress — more meaningful than income, because it captures what you've actually built and kept. Tracking it annually reveals whether you're truly moving forward, regardless of how much you earn or spend.

### How to Use it
List each asset and liability category. Update this calculation once or twice a year to track your financial trajectory over time.

### Note
Real estate and gold values should be estimated at current market price, not purchase price, for an accurate picture.
