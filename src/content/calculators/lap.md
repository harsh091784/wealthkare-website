### What is it?
A Loan Against Property lets you borrow against a residential or commercial property you own, at rates lower than personal loans, for any purpose — business expansion, education, medical needs, or debt consolidation.

### The Formula
Lenders typically finance 50-75% of the property's market value (Loan-to-Value ratio); the EMI is then calculated on this eligible loan amount using standard formula.

### Why it Matters
LAP offers access to significant capital at secured-loan rates (typically 9-14%, far below personal loan rates of 14-24%), making it an efficient way to unlock liquidity from an owned property without selling it.

### How to Use it
Enter your property's current market value, the LTV ratio your lender offers, and the rate/tenure. The calculator shows your borrowing capacity and monthly obligation.

### Assumptions
LTV ratios and rates vary by lender, property type, and location. Property valuation is typically conducted by the lender's approved valuer. Illustrative only.
