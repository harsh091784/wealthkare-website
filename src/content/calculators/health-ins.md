### What is it?
A calculator recommending an adequate health insurance cover based on your city, age, family size, and health profile — accounting for the sharply rising cost of medical treatment in India, especially in metro cities.

### The Formula
A base cover recommendation scaled by city tier and age band, with additions for family size and a buffer for pre-existing conditions that increase likely claim amounts.

### Why it Matters
Medical inflation in India runs at 12-15% annually — well above general inflation. A cover that felt adequate five years ago is often insufficient today. Underinsurance means a single hospitalization can force liquidation of long-term investments.

### How to Use it
Enter your city tier, age, family size, and existing cover. The calculator shows your recommended cover and the gap to fill — ideally through a combination of a base policy and a super top-up.

### Note
These are general guidelines, not medical or insurance advice. Actual premium depends on insurer, specific health conditions, and policy features. Illustrative only.
