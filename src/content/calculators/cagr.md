### What is it?
The Compound Annual Growth Rate tells you the smoothed annual rate at which an investment grew — as if it had grown at a steady rate every year, even though actual returns fluctuate.

### The Formula
$$CAGR = \left(\frac{\text{Final Value}}{\text{Initial Value}}\right)^{\frac{1}{\text{years}}} - 1$$

### Why it Matters
CAGR is the honest way to compare investments over different periods. An investment that doubled in 5 years has a CAGR of 14.87% — knowing this lets you compare it fairly against any other investment, fixed deposit, or benchmark.

### How to Use it
Enter what you invested, what it became, and how many years it took. The result is your annualized return.

### Assumptions
CAGR smooths out volatility — it does not show the journey, only the destination. Two investments with identical CAGR can have very different risk profiles.
