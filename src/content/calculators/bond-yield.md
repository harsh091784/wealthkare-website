### What is it?
A calculator that shows the actual return a bond investor earns based on its market price — which is often different from its stated coupon rate once the bond trades above or below its face value.

### The Formula
Current Yield is the annual coupon divided by the price you actually paid. Yield to Maturity (YTM) also factors in the gain or loss you'll realize if you hold the bond to maturity and it returns to face value.

### Why it Matters
A bond's coupon rate is fixed at issuance, but its market price moves with interest rates — when rates rise, bond prices fall, and yields rise, and vice versa. Understanding this inverse relationship is essential before buying or selling a bond in the secondary market.

### How to Use it
Enter the bond's face value, coupon rate, current market price, and years remaining to maturity. Compare the current yield and YTM against comparable fixed-income options.

### Assumptions
YTM shown is an approximation; the exact YTM requires iterative calculation. Assumes the issuer does not default and the bond is held to maturity. Illustrative only.
