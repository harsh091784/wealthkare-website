### What is it?
A one-time investment compounded over time. If you have a windfall — a bonus, a maturity payout, an inheritance — and want to put it to work in one shot rather than spreading it over months.

### The Formula
FV = P × (1+r)^n, where P = investment amount, r = annual return rate, n = years.

### Why it Matters
Time is the multiplier. ₹5 lakhs invested at 11% for 10 years becomes over ₹14 lakhs. At 20 years, it becomes over ₹40 lakhs — the same ₹5 lakhs, just more time.

### How to Use it
Enter the amount you want to invest, the number of years, and your expected return. Compare results at different return assumptions to understand the range of outcomes.

### Assumptions
Assumes constant annual compounding. Actual returns will vary. Illustrative only.
