### What is it?
A SIP where you increase your monthly contribution by a fixed percentage every year — say 10% annually. As your income grows, your investments grow with it. Small annual increases make a dramatic difference to your final corpus.

### The Formula
Each year's monthly SIP = previous year's SIP × (1 + step-up%). Each year's contributions are compounded forward to the end of the investment period.

### Why it Matters
Starting a ₹10,000/month SIP and stepping it up 10% each year gives you a far larger corpus than a flat ₹15,000/month SIP — with less strain on your budget in the early years.

### How to Use it
Enter your starting SIP, annual step-up percentage, duration, and expected return. Compare the result against a flat SIP to see the step-up advantage.

### Assumptions
Step-up is applied annually at the start of each year. Returns assumed constant. Illustrative only.
