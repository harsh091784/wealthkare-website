### What is it?
House Rent Allowance (HRA) exemption reduces your taxable income if you receive HRA as part of your salary and pay rent for your accommodation. This calculator computes the exact exemption you are entitled to claim under the Old Tax Regime.

### The Formula
HRA exempt = minimum of three values: (a) Actual HRA received from employer; (b) 50% of basic salary if you live in a metro city (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai), or 40% for non-metro; (c) Actual rent paid minus 10% of basic salary. The lowest of these three is your eligible exemption.

### Why it Matters
HRA exemption can be one of the largest deductions available to salaried employees — often worth ₹1–3 lakhs or more annually for those in metro cities. Many employees under-claim this because they don't know the three-part formula.

### How to Use it
Enter your monthly basic salary, actual HRA received, monthly rent paid, and whether you live in a metro city. The calculator shows your eligible annual HRA exemption and the taxable portion of your HRA.

### Note
HRA exemption is only available under the Old Tax Regime. If you have opted for the New Regime, HRA cannot be claimed. You must also be paying actual rent — HRA exemption cannot be claimed if you live in your own home. Illustrative only.
