### What is it?
A calculator that makes the true cost of procrastination visible — showing exactly how much wealth is lost by delaying the start of an investment, even by a few years.

### The Formula
Two SIP projections are compared for the same target end date — one starting today, one starting after your specified delay — isolating the cost of the lost years.

### Why it Matters
Because compounding accelerates over time, the years lost to delay are disproportionately expensive — often the most valuable years of the entire investment period. A 5-year delay on a 20-year SIP doesn't cost 25% of the corpus; it frequently costs 40-50%, because those were the years the base compounded the longest.

### How to Use it
Enter your planned SIP, total time horizon, and how many years you might delay starting. The "Amount Lost by Waiting" figure is designed to be a wake-up call — use it as such.

### Assumptions
Both scenarios assume the same constant return rate. Real markets fluctuate, but the structural cost of delay holds regardless of the exact rate used. Illustrative only.
