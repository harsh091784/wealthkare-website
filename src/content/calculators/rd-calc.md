### What is it?
A Recurring Deposit lets you invest a fixed amount every month with a bank for a fixed tenure at a guaranteed interest rate. Think of it as a fixed deposit that you build month by month — disciplined savings with a guaranteed return.

### The Formula
RD maturity uses quarterly compounding per RBI norms: M = R × [(1+r)^n – 1] / (1 – (1+r)^(–1/3)), where R = monthly deposit, r = quarterly interest rate, n = number of quarters.

### Why it Matters
RDs are ideal for people who want to save regularly without market risk. They are particularly useful for short-to-medium term goals (1–5 years) where capital safety matters more than returns. Like FDs, interest is taxable as per your slab.

### How to Use it
Enter your monthly deposit amount, tenure, and interest rate. The calculator shows your maturity amount and total interest earned.

### Assumptions
Quarterly compounding per RBI standard. Interest rate assumed constant. Interest income is taxable. Illustrative only.
