### What is it?
The Employees' Provident Fund is a mandatory retirement savings scheme for salaried employees in India. Both you and your employer contribute a fixed percentage of your basic salary every month, and the accumulated corpus earns a government-declared interest rate, compounding until retirement.

### The Formula
Employee contributes 12% of basic monthly; Employer contributes 3.67% to EPF + 8.33% to EPS (capped). Compound monthly at EPF rate, with basic salary increasing annually by the increment rate.

### Why it Matters
EPF is often an employee's largest guaranteed retirement asset without them realizing it. A ₹50,000 basic salary growing at 8% annually, contributing till 58, can build a corpus of ₹1.5–2 crores through compounding alone — money most people forget to count when planning retirement.

### How to Use it
Enter your basic + DA, current age, retirement age, any existing EPF balance, and expected annual increments. The calculator projects your EPF corpus at retirement.

### Assumptions
EPS (pension) portion is calculated separately by EPFO based on pensionable salary and service — not included in this corpus figure. Interest rate is revised annually by EPFO; current default is illustrative. Illustrative only.
