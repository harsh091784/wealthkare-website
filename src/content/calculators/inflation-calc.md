### What is it?
A calculator that shows you the real purchasing power of money over time — what a sum of money today will be worth in the future after inflation erodes it, and what you would need to invest to stay ahead of inflation.

### The Formula
Future cost = Present value × (1 + inflation rate)^years. Real value of today's money in the future = Present value ÷ (1 + inflation rate)^years.

### Why it Matters
Inflation is the most underestimated risk in financial planning. ₹10 lakhs today, at 6% inflation, has the purchasing power of only ₹5.6 lakhs in 10 years. What costs ₹10 lakhs today will cost ₹17.9 lakhs in 10 years. Staying in a savings account earning 3–4% while inflation runs at 6% means you are losing wealth in real terms every year.

### How to Use it
Enter a current amount, the inflation rate, and the number of years. The calculator shows the future cost of that amount and the real value of your money. A second line shows what the same amount would become if invested at 12% — the contrast makes the cost of inaction clear.

### Assumptions
Inflation rate is assumed constant. Actual inflation varies year to year. CPI inflation in India has averaged 5–7% over the past decade. Illustrative only.
