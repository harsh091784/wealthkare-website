### What is it?
A goal-based calculator that estimates how much your child's education will cost by the time they reach college age — and how much you need to invest monthly today to fund it.

### The Formula
Future education cost = Current cost × (1 + education inflation)^years until education. Then: reverse SIP to find the monthly investment needed to accumulate that amount.

### Why it Matters
Education costs in India are rising at 10–12% per year — far faster than general inflation. A course that costs ₹10 lakhs today will cost ₹25+ lakhs in 10 years. Starting early, even with small amounts, makes a significant difference.

### How to Use it
Enter your child's current age, the age at which they'll need the funds, the current estimated cost of their education, and your expected investment return. The calculator shows the future cost and the monthly SIP required.

### Assumptions
Uses 10% education inflation as the default — revise based on the specific type of education planned. Returns are assumed constant. Illustrative only.
