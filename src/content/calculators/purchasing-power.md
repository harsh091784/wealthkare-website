### What is it?
While the Inflation Impact calculator shows what things will cost in the future, this calculator flips the lens — showing what your money sitting idle today will actually be "worth" in real terms years from now.

### The Formula
Real Value = Amount ÷ (1 + inflation rate)^years — the mirror image of the future cost calculation.

### Why it Matters
₹10 lakhs sitting in a savings account earning 3-4% loses real value every single year that inflation runs at 6%. This calculator visualizes that erosion directly against what the same money could become if invested — making the cost of "safety" in a low-yield account viscerally clear.

### How to Use it
Enter an amount you're currently holding in cash or low-yield instruments, the inflation rate, and a time horizon. Compare the eroding line against the growing line.

### Assumptions
Inflation and investment returns are assumed constant; actual figures vary year to year. Illustrative only.
